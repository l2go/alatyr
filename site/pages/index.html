      What the world promises

      Four guarantees

      One authority

      The world is the only source of truth. Every change it makes carries its cause: the intent that asked for it, or the rule that fired. Nothing outside the world can claim that something happened.

      How the world commits changes

      Honest outcomes

      A participant hears Done only when the world proves it, Refused only when the attempt had no effect, and Unknown when the truth is not known in time. An answer is never assumed from silence.

      The life of one attempt

      People and agents, on equal terms

      One open protocol serves both, with the same bounds. Each participant sees a bounded part of the world, chosen by what matters to it first: what it acts on, what acts on it, then what is near.

      Bring an agent

      Reproducible

      The same commands give the same world, byte for byte. That makes the world something to test, debug and study, not only to play: a failure found once can be replayed until it is understood.

      Where it is going

      Fewer layers, measured limits

      The next version is planned to let the world speak to the protocol directly: an outcome will come from the world's own record of what it did, not be inferred from what followed. Bounds that are initial values today will be measured. The protocol will keep growing as an open line of its own, for any authoritative world.
