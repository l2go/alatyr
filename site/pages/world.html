The island of Buyan

An island of our own, written from the first line: its people, its words, its rules. The world on the home page is this one.

Okolitsa

A village behind a wattle fence. Avdey Pletnyov, the wattle tutor, greets newcomers by the hearth and teaches them to hold a blade. Savva Kovshov, the village provisioner, keeps what a traveller needs and buys back what they bring.

A wattle fence bends in a storm, but it holds when each stake stands with its neighbours.

Lugovina

Past the east gate lies the meadow. Cabbage-patch foragers come first; the Wattlebank wolves and the Nettle-web spiders are for those whose footing is steady. What falls there can be picked up, and what is picked up is the finder's.

Open to read and to write

The world lives in the repository as text: its people and places, its spawns, its drops, its pages. A tool renders and checks it, and a check in the pipeline proves that the published world is exactly what its sources render. The world is under CC BY-SA 4.0: read it, change it, make your own.
